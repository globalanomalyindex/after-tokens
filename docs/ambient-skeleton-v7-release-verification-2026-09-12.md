## Evidence boundaries

The retained 180 ms fit and 280 ms handover are authored parameters, not observed guarantees. The natural narrow sky-blue case demonstrates the tradeoff of a fixed waiting allocation: fitting precedes the retained handover, reaching near-full opacity at 400 ms and rest at 500 ms in this observation. V6’s zero-fit cases are not imported as v7 evidence.

Source eligibility, first DOM text, first visible ink, near-full opacity and complete rest are different timestamps. Geometry sampling and video capture impose overhead. Local glyph stability does not imply zero viewport movement. Emulation is not a physical iPhone, exact output is not factual correctness, and no reader study has been run.
