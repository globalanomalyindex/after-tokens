# After Tokens: anchored skeleton motion

> Historical v5 record (`adaptive-cell-skeleton-v5`, published at `06da788390549574949c7cdcd938c3669820f214`). Its parameters, reports and captures describe that material; they do not validate the later v6 bubble-to-word handover.

**9 September 2026 · material `adaptive-cell-skeleton-v5` · design rationale and testable hypotheses.**

One rounded capsule divides into a field that gradually makes room for the answer. Solid rows provide references while smaller pills appear and their neighbors redistribute space. A shared breath and occasional faint glimmer continue. The waiting height uses a capped estimate from available source content and the container’s type metrics; individual cells never map to real words. Committed positions and revisable candidates retain different source meanings.

After actual source finality, the renderer can measure the true answer. If the current frame is too small, a nominal 180 ms size fit precedes full visual availability; otherwise the answer appears immediately while extra space shrinks. All words then share one small 180 ms settling motion at full opacity, accompanied by a 260 ms outline. This adds an explicit presentation cost. The measurements below report actual delay and initial glyph travel, rather than inherit the previous immediate-visibility or zero-movement claims.

## A causal estimate, not a preview

The source contract determines the available sizing input. For the recorded Qwen source, the estimator reads only current committed token fragments. It excludes the earliest currently committed end token and all later positions, even when that end precedes the contiguous prefix. That path does not read drafts, snapshot candidates, future words, a hidden future answer, final Markdown, received-token totals or the source’s capacity bound. Known newline characters contribute to the estimate; they do not establish a complete format.

If an integration actually supplies revisable whole snapshots, a separate estimator can measure its latest nonfinal candidate to prepare approximate space. The candidate remains provisional: it does not populate committed tokens, the prefix or released passages, and its words do not enter the protected reading surface. Candidate storage clears on finality, stop, error, revision and reset. A final-only integration has no intermediate sizing evidence and retains the five-row initial allocation until actual finality. No production Gemini snapshot adapter is claimed.

For the usable container width `W`, sum the available content’s measured advance as `A` and count its newline breaks as `N`. The committed path measures current fragments; the snapshot path measures lines in the current candidate. Both use the authored heuristic:

```text
rows = clamp(5, 14, ceil(1.1 × A / W + 0.5 × N) + 1)
```

The 1.1 packing factor, half-line newline contribution, one-row slack and 5–14 row limits are design parameters, not fitted results or mathematical bounds. Fragment advances do not reproduce whole-text shaping, word wrapping, unseen spaces or future content. Revisable candidates may contain noise, lose lines or grow; their current extent is especially weak evidence of final size. The estimate can under- or overallocate. An exact future line count would require unavailable information.

Within one run and a stable width/font context, the waiting row maximum only grows. This avoids shrinking and regrowing the envelope when the estimate changes. A real width, font, line-height or letter-spacing context change permits a new estimate. Finality always replaces the estimate with the measured final page height, including shrinking excessive space.

Rows follow the answer’s actual computed line-height, and bars are 0.9 times its font size. The waiting frame’s target is `rows × line-height`, animated from its current rendered height over a nominal 380 ms. All 14 capped rows are mounted from the beginning; only the numeric estimate exposes additional rows, so new cells retain the original decorative clocks. Individual pill widths and positions remain authored.

## Implemented movement grammar

Still, breathe and reshape retain common source eligibility and final handover rules. Still holds the authored pill arrangement; breathe adds the 4.8-second pulse. Reshape combines the capsule introduction, local redistribution, appearance/settle and intermittent glimmer. All three use the adaptive waiting estimate and final answer-size handoff. Their initial presentation differs, and actual visual arrival must be measured separately from the common source deadline.

| Row material | Repeating width pattern 88%, 92%, 96%, 84%, 70%; left zero; bars 0.9 × font size, centered in each actual line-height | Aligns the field with the text container and typographic rhythm, without matching future words. |

The division occupies the initial five-line region. Five contiguous slabs start at left 4%, width 88% and height 0.56 × font size, centered together vertically. Their joined top is `(5 × line-height − 2.8 × font-size) / 2`. They separate into the first five row centers. The 1.8% overshoot applies to top, width and the height change; horizontal offsets stay between 4% and zero. During 750–950 ms, the division’s opacity falls from 0.2 to zero while the field rises from zero to one. The hidden division remains inert until the composition is removed.

All decorative clocks begin together with active motion; cluster and glimmer animation run behind the introduction. The first glimmer sweep occupies 1.28–2.08 seconds of decorative elapsed time. The 4,800 and 8,000 ms cycles scale with the existing tempo input; the introduction remains 950 ms. Source replay and decorative time can diverge through pause and visibility controls. Additional rows fade in over 220 ms without remounting their animation layers.

The estimate, introduction, pulse, changing widths, translation, transient scale, row/pill presence, glimmer and final handoff all alter the stimulus. Still versus breathe and breathe versus reshape remain combined treatment comparisons, not proofs of an isolated perceptual mechanism. Static’s nominal opacity midpoint does not match every finite-exposure ink average.

## Source finality and visual readiness are different events

A committed end reached by the contiguous prefix, a valid explicit finish or an explicitly final snapshot establishes source finality. Animation cannot establish it. Only then can the actual final text enter the measurement page. During a necessary size fit it is hidden from view; the status says “answer received · fitting the view.” Retained cells now indicate presentation preparation, not continuing model work.

The renderer does not wait for division, a pulse or glimmer to finish. A short fit may interrupt them at any phase. With enough room, full text becomes visible immediately while the frame shrinks. Motion-off, reduced motion, pause, offscreen or hidden conditions bypass the decorative fit and arrival movement and use the exact final height directly. A width/font change during fitting also finishes immediately rather than restarting the waiting budget. A new run invalidates old fit callbacks; later visible reflow does not add a second arrival cue.

Initial reduced motion still freezes the joined capsule during waiting. This is separate from the finality bypass: once the source is complete, it reaches visible text without the fit or settling animation. Completion decoration must not replay merely because motion is reenabled or the surface becomes visible.

## Engineering observations and remaining evidence

Eight [instrumented Chromium observations](anchored-skeleton-motion-validation-2026-09-09.json) preserve exact final output with no sampled pre-final protected text. The longer sky-blue example grows from five to ten waiting rows, then reconciles to a smaller actual answer. The authored arrival produces an observed range of approximately 1.7 px; after settling, 7,330 matched word-first-glyph samples show zero displacement. This supports the local contracts for adaptation, whole-answer arrival and subsequent stability. It does not establish that readers prefer the motion.

All eight cases had sufficient space at finality and exposed text in the same sampled frame as source completion. They do not measure the delayed underallocation branch or establish zero real-world latency. The dense 43-cell inspection produces loading rAF-gap p95 values of 150.9–216.7 ms, including in still; inspection and recording overhead are not separated from runtime cost. Diagnostic recordings are retained as measurement evidence. Two separately archived [passive presentation captures](anchored-skeleton-showcase-capture-2026-09-09.json) illustrate the treatment without dense per-frame sampling, but cannot prove physical-device smoothness. Full scope and artifacts are in the [release record](anchored-skeleton-release-verification-2026-09-09.md).

A separate [forced-fit test](anchored-skeleton-fit-stress-2026-09-09.json) starts with an active five-row frame and immediately forces the sky-blue fixture’s final event. The observed additional wait is 200.1 ms in Chromium, 204 ms in WebKit and 195 ms in iPhone 14 emulation, with the same cells retained and the full text hidden during fitting. All three show zero post-settle movement. This confirms the branch and exposes its actual sampled presentation cost in one trial per profile. It is an artificial finality trigger, not captured model timing, a latency distribution or a physical-device measurement.

Measure actual source finality, full visual availability and the end of arrival motion separately. A nominal 180 ms fit is not a strict wall-clock upper bound. Report frame growth before finality, residual correction after finality, peak sampled height changes, initial glyph travel and post-settle stability. Include overestimates, underestimates, the fourteen-row cap, long unbroken strings, newlines and final snapshots without intermediate commitments.

Also verify forbidden future-data access, EOS exclusion, safe row limits, distinct committed and snapshot sizing paths, candidate cleanup and no candidate-word release. Include a correct-looking nonfinal snapshot and a final-only source. Verify preserved glimmer phase on added rows, bounded division, row endpoints, neighbor gaps, pause/reduced-motion behavior and stale-callback cancellation. Old fixed-height and zero-arrival-movement results do not validate this adaptive material.

The division metaphor and word-like shapes may cause readers to infer decoder stages, exact words or progress. A size estimate may strengthen that mistaken impression. Ask what the shapes imply before explaining them. No source here proves increased dopamine, trust, comprehension or preference, and a polished arrival says nothing about answer correctness.

## Reader study and decision rule

The first study should compare reshape with breathe using matched source durations and the same finality policy. Counterbalance stimuli and include short, long, malformed, refused and interrupted output. Record actual visual delay and final height correction rather than assuming identical browser arrival from shared source timing. Ask readers what the bars mean before explaining the treatment. Record fluidity and distraction, perceived wait, expected word and line count, completion estimates and preference after repeated exposure. The primary measure and smallest useful effect should be chosen after a pilot, then used to power and preregister a confirmatory study. No sample size or result is invented here.

A softer-looking animation is insufficient if it increases distraction or creates false confidence in a wrong answer. Breathe or still remains a valid outcome and can become the default. Compare earlier-word access with whole-answer release separately: the unchanged original-corpus report finds a paired median 10.5-second additional wait over sentence release across 57 nonempty runs. That is measured source-reducer eligibility, not a psychological benefit or production API latency. [Availability report](../data/experiments/answer-policy-cost-2026-09-09.json).
