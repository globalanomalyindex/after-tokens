## Evidence boundaries retained

The Google archive demonstrates distributed visible revision, not a public snapshot API, exact early formatting or an inference clock. Current committed positions and actually received snapshots support different approximate geometry contracts; neither reveals future words. Final-only input has no intermediate content evidence.

Browser geometry and lifecycle checks can establish implementation behavior. They cannot establish comfort, comprehension, perceived wait, preference, mistaken trust, dopamine effects or novelty priority. Those remain reader-study questions. Still/breathe/reshape compare a combined waiting treatment; the shared handover requires a separate ablation to isolate its effect.

Fresh v6 captures and measurement summaries identify their own material and 12 September date. The presentation examples were replaced only after their files and reports existed; first ink, near-full opacity and settled/unoccluded access are reported separately. Publication status belongs to a commit-specific release record, not this copy audit.
